Effective Date: October 22, 2026 | Last Updated: October 22, 2026

A.E. CCTV AND COMPUTER INSTALLATION SERVICES, operating under the software brand ASV Solutions ("we", "us", or "our"), develops and operates BANAAG ("the Application"). We are dedicated to protecting your personal privacy.

1. Zero Personal Data Collection (Offline-First Engine)

BANAAG is engineered as an offline-first professional engineering application. We do not require account registration, do not collect personal identifying information (PII), and do not track or sell user behavioral data.

2. Device Storage & Local Calculations

All electrical formulas, branch circuit schedules, Single-Line Diagrams, and Bill of Materials (BOM) estimates created within BANAAG are calculated and stored locally on your device's internal storage using SQLite/encrypted local preferences. You maintain 100% control and ownership over your engineering data.

3. File Storage & Export Permissions

The Application may request permission to access device storage solely to allow you to save, export, or share generated PDF engineering reports and Single-Line Diagram schematics. No other files on your device are read or accessed.

4. Third-Party Services

The standard offline release does not integrate third-party ad networks, telemetry trackers, or behavioral analytics SDKs.
