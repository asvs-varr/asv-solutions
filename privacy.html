cash · flow · save Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 17, 2026 | Provided by ASV Solution

Core Privacy Philosophy: cash · flow · save is designed with an offline-first architecture. Your financial records, schedules, income, and expense logs belong entirely to you.

1. Information We Collect

We believe in data minimization. We only collect the minimal information necessary for the application to function:

  • Account Authentication Information: If you choose to enable cloud backup via Google Sign-In, we collect your basic Google account identifier (such as your User ID and email address) managed securely by Google Firebase Authentication.
  • Financial & Transactional Records: User-inputted financial data including amounts, transaction categories, payment modes, descriptions, recurring schedules, and optional vendor or tax references (e.g. BIR notes).
  • Device & Storage Data: Local storage permissions to save and read your transaction ledger locally on your device for fast offline access.

2. How Your Data is Used

Your data is used strictly for the following purposes:

  • To compute, organize, and display your income, expenses, accounts, and bill schedules within the app.
  • To securely synchronize your encrypted personal ledger with your private Google Firebase Firestore database when cloud backup is active.
  • To provide immediate-attention alerts for upcoming or overdue bills.

3. Third-Party Services & Data Sharing

We do NOT sell, rent, monetize, or share your personal or financial data with any third-party advertisers or data brokers.

The app integrates only trusted infrastructure providers for authentication and optional database synchronization:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication & Cloud Firestore): Governed by Google Privacy & Terms.
  • Google Play Services: Required for secure application distribution and authentication integration.

4. Data Security & Storage

All transactional data saved locally is restricted to the application's private sandboxed sandbox storage. When cloud synchronization is active, all communication is transmitted via standard Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS) encryption, with access strictly restricted to your authenticated user account.

5. Data Retention & Account Deletion

You have full autonomy over your data:

  • You can edit or delete any transaction, schedule, or account directly from within the app at any time.
  • Uninstalling the app without cloud sync permanently deletes all local records.
  • To request complete deletion of cloud-synchronized records associated with your account, you can contact us at our support email below.

6. Children's Privacy

cash · flow · save does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any updates will be published directly on this page with an updated effective date.
